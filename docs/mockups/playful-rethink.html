Simata — rethink the look, not just the theme

Two full appearance systems. Each changes the layout language, typography, card mechanics and illustration — not only the colour. Same screens and same rules underneath.

A · The Long Road. Turns the app into a journey to the test. A warm paper palette, a hand-drawn road with the car where you are and the exam as the destination, today's practice as a vertical route of stops, and the quiz as a real paper flashcard (taped, slightly askew, with a "NICE" stamp when you get one). The summary is a trip log on ruled paper.

B · Deck. Treats the question bank as a physical deck. Bright colour blocks, huge editorial numbers, mode cards instead of buttons, and a quiz card that visibly sits on a stack with an index tab. The summary is a scoreboard. Playful through scale, colour and card mechanics.

Both keep: answer-first/sort-second, thumb-reach sort bar, semantic colours in spirit (orange→hard, teal/green→easy, violet→guessed), streak, run summary, and the "no picture" slot. The main question is which language fits Simata.
